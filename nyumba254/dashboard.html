Charging a finder's fee for rentals

Remember: for rental listings, it's fair to ask tenants for a small one-off finder's fee once they move in, since they found the home through Nyumba254 instead of an agent. Agree on the amount upfront, keep it reasonable, and never request payment before the tenant has viewed and confirmed the unit. This fee is settled directly between you and the tenant — Nyumba254 doesn't collect it, and tenants can report requests that feel excessive.
